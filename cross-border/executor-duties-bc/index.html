先清点，再认证

第一步是找到遗嘱的最新版本，列出全部资产与债务：银行与投资账户、房产的持有方式、指定了受益人的保险与注册账户、国内的房产与存款。带生存者权利的联名资产、有效指定了受益人的保险与注册账户，一般直接转给对方而不经过遗嘱；是不是这样，要看登记方式和指定是否仍然有效。
持有逝者资产的机构和金融机构，可以要求先办遗嘱认证（probate）才肯办理。认证可以向 BC 最高法院任何一处登记处申请，表格多、要列明资产价值；不确定要不要认证、该怎样办理的，可以先请教律师。

常见情形

父亲去世，房子与母亲以带生存者权利的方式联名，存款在他一人名下。房子通常直接归母亲；存款要经遗嘱认证。转给母亲的财产一般不会在最终报税表上产生资本利得，但最终报税表照样要报。
执行人身在外地，兄弟姐妹催着分钱。先报税、拿清税证明再分配；提前分了钱，日后税务局追税时，执行人要在已分出去的金额以内自己承担。
遗产里有一套出租房和国内的存款。出租房去世时视同按市值出售，增值进最终报税表；国内存款按国内程序办，加拿大这边仍要申报。

An executor's work falls into roughly three stages: take inventory and obtain probate, file returns and clear taxes, and only then distribute. Get the order wrong and the executor may have to cover the estate's unpaid tax personally.

17 figures and rules on this page, last checked 2026-11-04 · Written by our editorial team · Editorially approved 2026-11-04

After a death in the family, the executor named in the will is responsible for getting the estate to the beneficiaries. The basic duties listed by the BC government are: list and value assets and debts, notify family and beneficiaries, take control of the assets, collect money owed, pay debts, file returns for the deceased and the estate, distribute the estate and keep accounts

Take inventory first, then obtain probate

The first step is to find the latest version of the will and list all assets and debts: bank and investment accounts, how real estate is held, insurance and registered accounts with a named beneficiary, and property and deposits in China. Jointly held assets with right of survivorship, and insurance and registered accounts with a valid beneficiary designation, generally pass directly to the other person without going through the will; whether that is so depends on how they are registered and whether the designation is still valid.
Organizations and financial institutions holding the deceased's assets may require probate before they will act. Probate can be applied for at any registry of the Supreme Court of British Columbia; there are many forms, and asset values must be stated. If you are not sure whether probate is needed or how to go about it, ask a lawyer first.

How probate fees are calculated

Probate fees are calculated on only part of the property, namely property passing to the personal representative: real property and tangible personal property in BC, plus, for a person ordinarily resident in BC before death, intangible personal property wherever it is; application and filing fees under the court rules are extra

Common situations

A father dies. The house was held with the mother as joint tenants with right of survivorship; the deposits were in his name alone. The house usually goes straight to the mother; the deposits have to go through probate. Property passing to the mother generally produces no capital gain on the final return, but the final return must still be filed.
The executor lives elsewhere and siblings are pressing for the money. File the returns and get a clearance certificate before distributing; if money is paid out early and the CRA later collects tax, the executor is personally responsible up to the amount already paid out.
The estate has a rental unit and deposits in China. The rental is deemed sold at market value on death and the gain goes into the final return; the deposits in China follow the Chinese procedure and must still be reported in Canada.
