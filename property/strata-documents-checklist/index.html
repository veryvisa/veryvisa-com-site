Knowledge cards

Knowledge cards

The set of strata documents

Decisions, finances, works, rules and insurance

  1. Meeting minutes
  2. Budget and financial statements
  3. Contingency reserve fund and depreciation report
  4. Form B and statement of fees
  5. Bylaws, rules and insurance certificate

Look first: Put several years of minutes in date order before reading.

Easy to miss: The seller's package may be missing the most recent decisions.

Next step: Cross-check special levies between Form B and the latest minutes.

Takeaway: No single document shows the whole building.

Knowledge cards

Risk signals in the documents

A recurring problem matters more than a single repair request

  1. Repeated leaks
  2. Envelope and roof works
  3. Elevator and piping renewal
  4. Insurance claims and high deductibles
  5. Lawsuits and special levies

Look first: Follow one matter from the time it is raised, through quotes, to the vote.

Easy to miss: A large reserve balance may still fall short of major works coming soon.

Next step: Have your realtor and lawyer add undecided works to the risk list.

Takeaway: Read risk from the trend, not from one isolated line.

Common situations

The minutes mention the same leak again and again, without a clear repair plan. Keep asking for the engineering report, quotes, insurance claim and source of funds.
The reserve fund looks sufficient, but the depreciation report lists several major works soon. Put expected spending next to current funds, then judge the risk of a special levy.
The buyer plans to rent out the unit or keep a pet. Besides Form B, read the current bylaws, the rules and recent amendments passed but not yet consolidated.
