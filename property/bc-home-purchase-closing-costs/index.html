How property transfer tax is banded

The general tax is calculated in bands on the property's fair market value: 1% (up to CA$200,000); 2% (above CA$200,000, up to CA$2,000,000); 3% (above CA$2,000,000); a further 2% on residential value over CA$3,000,000
