Ways for children to stay in Canada after graduating

There is no automatic track from graduation to permanent residence: work permit, work experience, federal invitation and provincial nomination each have to be checked separately.

33 figures and rules on this page, last checked 2026-10-17 · Written by our editorial team · Review in progress

A common path is to obtain a post-graduation work permit, build up qualifying Canadian work experience, and then enter Express Entry or apply for provincial nomination. Every step has its own conditions.
The work experience, occupation level and time window required by the Canadian Experience Class are: at least 1 year (1,560 hours) of skilled work experience in Canada in NOC TEER 0–3, gained within the 3 years before applying

. After meeting the basic requirements, you still have to enter the pool and wait for an invitation.
Provincial graduate and employer categories are adjusted to each province's needs. Choose a school and program only on current official rules; do not treat a program stream that is open today as a promise for after graduation.

A post-graduation work permit is the first gate

First confirm that the school and the exact program meet the PGWP conditions, then plan status during study, graduation documents, language requirements and the application window. Without suitable work authorization, the experience-based routes that follow are affected too.
A field of study does not automatically match a future occupation code. Choose courses, internships and jobs so as to build experience in actual duties that can be proven.

Provincial nomination depends on the province's needs at the time

Knowledge cards

Knowledge cards

From study to permanent residence

Every step has its own conditions

  1. Eligible school and program
  2. Post-graduation work permit
  3. Qualifying work experience
  4. The Express Entry pool
  5. Federal invitation or provincial nomination

Look first: Work back from post-graduation work authorization to today's choice of school and program.

Easy to miss: Qualifying for an experience class does not guarantee an invitation.

Next step: For each step, write down the official entry point and a fallback route.

Takeaway: Plan through several gates; do not bet on one policy.

Knowledge cards

A file of documents for the pathway

Keep study, status and work records without gaps

  1. Admission and enrolment documents
  2. Transcripts and completion letter
  3. Study permit and work permit
  4. Contract, pay and tax slips
  5. Evidence of duties and occupation code

Look first: Keep the official document from each change of status or job.

Easy to miss: A job title cannot replace proof of actual duties.

Next step: Review the pathway against current rules regularly, not only at graduation.

Takeaway: Continuous evidence matters as much as continuous status.

Common situations

A child plans to take a college program. First check the PGWP and field-of-study requirements, then see whether the province of graduation has a lasting employer-based route.
A PGWP has been obtained, but the job duties do not match the job title. Confirm the occupation code by the actual main duties and keep employer letters and pay records.
The basic conditions of the Canadian Experience Class are met, but the ranking is below recent invitation cut-offs. Check provincial nomination at the same time, and do not mistake pool eligibility for an invitation.
