把未还债务、家人未来生活费、子女教育与身后费用列在一边，把可立即使用的资产、现有寿险与确定能领的公共福利列在另一边，两边之差才是保障缺口。
牛奶金与 CPP 遗属福利都要先核资格，不能把最高额直接当作家庭一定会收到的钱；收入、孩子年龄与供款记录都会改变结果。
这套算法只帮助整理前提与缺口，不选择产品。最终保额、保障期限与保单组合由持牌保险顾问完成需求分析后说明。

先列必须继续支付的责任

房贷与其他债务写当前余额；家庭生活费按失去收入后仍需维持的部分计算；子女教育、照护与身后费用分开列，避免重复。
每项责任都要有结束条件。房贷还清、孩子独立与配偶退休的时间不同，保障期限也不应只用一个笼统年份。

再扣除能够使用的资源

应急储蓄、可变现投资、雇主团体寿险与已有个人保单可以抵减缺口；自住房、退休账户或专作其他用途的资产，不一定适合全部计入。

常见情形

双职工家庭仍有房贷，孩子尚未独立。分别计算两位收入者离世后的缺口，不能只用家庭总收入乘一个倍数。
雇主提供团体寿险。先看离职后是否保留、保额是否随薪资变化，再决定能扣除多少。
家庭已有投资房与退休账户。先确认这些资产是否能及时变现、是否另有养老用途，再决定是否计入保障资源。

How to estimate life insurance coverage

For life insurance, first work out the cash gap the family would face after losing an income, then subtract the resources that are really available.

6 figures and rules on this page, last checked 2026-09-30 · Written by our editorial team · Review in progress

On one side, list outstanding debts, the family's future living costs, children's education and final expenses; on the other, assets available at once, existing life insurance and public benefits that are certain to be paid. The difference is the protection gap.
Eligibility for both the Canada Child Benefit and CPP survivor benefits must be checked first; the maximum cannot be taken as money the family will certainly receive. Income, the children's ages and the contribution record all change the result.
This method only helps organize the assumptions and the gap; it does not choose a product. The final amount, term and mix of policies are explained by a licensed insurance adviser after a needs analysis.

List obligations that must go on being paid

Enter mortgage and other debts at their current balance; count the living costs that must still be met after losing the income; list children's education, care and final expenses separately to avoid double counting.
Every obligation needs an end condition. The mortgage is paid off, the children become independent and the spouse retires at different times, so the term should not rest on one vague year.

Then subtract resources that can be used

Emergency savings, investments that can be sold, employer group life and existing personal policies can reduce the gap; a home, retirement accounts or assets set aside for other purposes may not all be suitable to count.

Knowledge cards

Knowledge cards

The family protection gap sheet

Obligations minus available resources

  1. Debts and mortgage
  2. Family living costs
  3. Children's education and care
  4. Final expenses
  5. Assets, existing coverage and benefits

Look first: Work out, for each earner separately, what the family would lose.

Easy to miss: A home and retirement accounts cannot always be fully used to fill an immediate gap.

Next step: For each obligation, write down the basis for the amount and when it ends.

Takeaway: Coverage comes from the gap, not from a multiple of income.

Knowledge cards

How public benefits count

Check eligibility first, then add to cash flow

  1. Contribution record
  2. Spouse's and children's eligibility
  3. Family net income
  4. When payments start
  5. Tax and review

Look first: Count only the benefits the family can be reasonably sure of receiving.

Easy to miss: The maximum on an official website is not the actual amount for every family.

Next step: List uncertain benefits separately as a sensitivity scenario.

Takeaway: Benefits can narrow the gap, but do not budget on the full maximum.

Common situations

A two-income family still has a mortgage and dependent children. Work out the gap after the death of each earner separately; do not just multiply total family income.
An employer provides group life. First see whether it continues after leaving the job and whether it changes with salary, then decide how much to subtract.
The family has an investment property and retirement accounts. First confirm whether these can be sold in time and whether they are meant for retirement, then decide whether to count them.
