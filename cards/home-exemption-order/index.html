The order for checking exemptions

Go through eligibility, price and use one by one

  1. Status and residency conditions
  2. Past record of owning a home
  3. The property's fair market value
  4. First-time buyer or newly built home conditions
  5. Residence requirement after registration

Look first: Check personal eligibility first, then place the price in the right band.

Easy to miss: Meeting the price threshold does not mean the other conditions are met automatically.

Next step: Have the professional handling the transfer confirm the filing documents.

Takeaway: An exemption is a set of conditions, not a single price line.
