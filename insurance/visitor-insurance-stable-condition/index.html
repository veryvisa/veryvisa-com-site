投保前把就医、检查、处方与调药记录按时间排好，再由持牌保险顾问对照问卷与原文说明；答案必须与医疗记录一致。

稳定通常核对哪些变化

保单常会核对是否出现新症状、原有症状是否加重、治疗方案是否改变、药物是否增减，以及是否安排新的检查或专科转诊。
不同条款对日常剂量调整、例行复诊与待查结果的处理并不相同，不能把一家公司的解释套到另一份保单。

逐格比较的顺序

  1. 先按投保人的年龄找到适用的稳定期。
  2. 再读稳定定义，逐项对照最近的病历与用药。
  3. 查看既往症保障是否包含在所选计划内。
  4. 若有不稳定既往症选项，继续核对适用疾病、等待安排与除外责任。
  5. 最后确认是否需要医疗问卷，以及谁负责回答。

投保与理赔用同一套事实

投保问卷、医生记录与理赔资料会相互核对。记不清药名或日期时，应先向药房和诊所索取记录，不凭印象填写。
条款比较只说明合同差异，不代表某一款适合所有家庭；保障选择要连同旅行时间、健康记录与可承受的自付额一起看。

常见情形

父亲长期服用同一种药，医生最近只调整了剂量。是否仍算稳定，要看具体保单怎样处理剂量变化。
母亲没有新症状，但正在等专科检查。待查结果是否打破稳定期，各家条款写法不同。
已经投保后才想起一次门诊记录。应尽快把原问卷与病历交给持牌顾问核对，不自行判断是否无关。

深读

Visitor medical insurance: how to check the pre-existing condition stability period

🎧 Listen (in Chinese): Visitor and travel medical insurance (about 20 minutes)

Whether a condition is stable is checked against the contract's definition of symptoms, treatment, medication and tests.

29 figures and rules on this page, last checked 2026-10-02 · Written by our editorial team · Review in progress

Whether a pre-existing condition is covered depends first on whether it continuously met the policy's definition of stable before the effective date. Feeling fine is not the same as stable in the policy's sense.
The stability period varies with product, age and coverage option. Visitor plans available in BC are listed below one by one, and blanks show clearly whether the policy states the point.
Before applying, put doctor visits, tests, prescriptions and medication changes in date order, then have a licensed insurance adviser go through the questionnaire and wording with you; answers must agree with the medical records.

Which changes are usually checked for stability

Policies often check for new symptoms, worsening of existing symptoms, changes in treatment, medication added or reduced, and new tests or specialist referrals.
Wordings differ on routine dose adjustments, regular follow-ups and pending results; one insurer's explanation cannot be applied to another policy.

The order for a clause-by-clause comparison

  1. First find the stability period that applies to the insured's age.
  2. Then read the definition of stable and compare it, point by point, with recent records and medication.
  3. See whether coverage for pre-existing conditions is included in the chosen plan.
  4. If there is an option for unstable pre-existing conditions, go on to check which conditions it applies to, waiting arrangements and exclusions.
  5. Finally, confirm whether a medical questionnaire is needed and who is responsible for answering it.

Use one set of facts for application and claim

The questionnaire, doctors' records and claim documents are checked against each other. If you cannot recall a drug name or date, ask the pharmacy and clinic for records first; do not fill it in from memory.
A comparison of wording shows only how contracts differ; it does not mean one product suits every family. Consider coverage together with the length of the trip, health records and the deductible you can bear.

Common situations

A father has long taken the same drug, and the doctor recently changed only the dose. Whether he is still stable depends on how the policy treats dose changes.
A mother has no new symptoms but is waiting for a specialist test. Wordings differ on whether a pending result breaks the stability period.
A clinic visit is remembered only after the policy was bought. Give the original questionnaire and records to a licensed adviser as soon as possible; do not decide on your own that it does not matter.
