超级签证保单核对清单

递交前逐项对照保单

  1. 承保公司是否符合资格
  2. 自入境起算的有效期是否足够
  3. 紧急医疗保额是否达标
  4. 是否涵盖医疗、住院与遣返
  5. 付款证明是否齐全

先看：先看保单上签发保单的公司，而不是销售方或经纪的名字。

易漏：保额按每位申请人计算，夫妻同行不能合用一份。

下一步：选分期付款时，看下一张卡核对退款。

带走：递交前，把保单与清单逐项对一遍。

分期付款的退款问题

四种情形分别核对

  1. 签证被拒
  2. 批签后未入境
  3. 提前离开加拿大
  4. 保单期内已有理赔

先看：拒签后提交退款申请的期限，各家写得不同。

易漏：分期产品常有不退的开单费与首期押金。

下一步：在下方表格中找到对应产品的退款原文。

带走：分期付款，退款条款要先读清楚。

常见情形

父母两人一同申请。每人各需一份符合要求的保单，保额分别计算。
保单买了一年，父母打算住得更久。保单在离开加拿大之前到期的，要及时续保，每次入境都要有有效保单。
已经批签，但因家中变故没有成行。未入境时的退款条件以保单原文为准，有的要求在生效日之前申请，或提供未入境的证明。

Knowledge cards

Checklist for a super visa policy

Compare the policy line by line before applying

  1. Does the insurer qualify?
  2. Is the policy valid long enough, counted from the date of entry?
  3. Does emergency medical coverage reach the required amount?
  4. Does it cover health care, hospitalization and repatriation?
  5. Is proof of payment complete?

Look first: Look at the company that issued the policy, not the seller or broker.

Easy to miss: Coverage is counted per applicant; a couple cannot share one policy.

Next step: If you choose to pay monthly, check refunds on the next card.

Takeaway: Before applying, go through the policy against the checklist line by line.

Knowledge cards

Refunds on monthly payment plans

Check four situations separately

  1. Visa refused
  2. Approved but never entered
  3. Left Canada early
  4. A claim already made in the policy term

Look first: Each insurer words its deadline for a refund request after a refusal differently.

Easy to miss: Monthly plans often have a non-refundable policy fee and an initial deposit.

Next step: Find the product's refund wording in the table below.

Takeaway: If you pay monthly, read the refund clause first.

Common situations

Two parents apply together. Each needs a policy that meets the requirements, with coverage counted separately.
A one-year policy was bought, but the parents plan to stay longer. If it expires before they leave Canada, renew it in time; a valid policy is needed for every entry.
The visa was approved, but a change in the family meant the trip did not happen. Refund conditions for not entering follow the policy wording; some require the request before the effective date, or proof of not having entered.
