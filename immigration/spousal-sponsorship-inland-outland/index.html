境内与境外申请的核心差别，在处理类别、共同居住、工签安排与拒签后的复核路径。

本页 7 个数字与规则，最近核对 2026-10-17 · 编辑部撰写 · 审定中

境内类别适用于在加拿大共同生活的配偶或同居伴侣；境外申请属于 Family Class。两条路径都要证明担保资格与关系真实。
境内类别的合资格申请人在永久居民申请审理期间可能申请开放工签；境外类别被拒后，担保人有专门的上诉路径。
选哪一类不能只看当前住址，还要考虑出入境、身份有效期与材料结构；收费的路径判断与代理服务由持牌移民顾问或律师提供。

Spousal sponsorship: inland and outland applications compared

The core differences between inland and outland applications lie in the class, living together, work permits and review after a refusal.

7 figures and rules on this page, last checked 2026-10-17 · Written by our editorial team · Review in progress

The inland class is for spouses or common-law partners living together in Canada; an outland application falls under the Family Class. Both routes require proof of sponsor eligibility and a genuine relationship.
A qualifying inland applicant may apply for an open work permit while permanent residence is processed; after an outland refusal, the sponsor has a dedicated appeal route.
Choosing a class cannot rest on current address alone; travel, validity of status and the structure of the documents also count. Paid advice on the route and representation must come from a licensed immigration consultant or lawyer.

What inland and outland mean

The inland class is processed by IRCC's Case Processing Centre in Mississauga: CPC-M (Case Processing Centre – Mississauga) handles and assesses the sponsorship and permanent residence applications; a case needing an interview or involving serious criminality may be transferred to an IRCC local office in Canada

Common situations

Applicant and sponsor are both in Canada and plan to keep living together during processing. Check the inland class and the open work permit conditions.
The applicant needs to leave Canada often to care for family. Assess documents for returning, risk to status and the class of application together.
There has been a refusal, a previous relationship or conflicting address records. Explain the facts and evidence first; do not pile up chat logs to cover a key contradiction.
