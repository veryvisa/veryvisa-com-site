学校合资格，项目仍要另核

先在 DLI 名单确认学校，再确认具体校区、项目与入学时点是否允许申请毕业工签。项目转学、远程学习与公私合作安排可能改变结论。
保存录取信、在读证明、成绩单与完成信。最终成绩发布日会启动申请窗口，不能只按毕业典礼日期计算。

语言与专业领域

非学位课程的现行语言门槛为 CLB/NCLC 5，四项能力均需达到

递交前的身份与工作授权

申请窗口与合法身份是两件事。原学签何时失效、是否在有效期内递交、毕业后何时停止按学生条件工作，都要按个人时间线核对。
材料不足或状态已经变化时，不要用一条通用说明推断能否继续工作。先保存递交回执与学校文件，再请持牌人士核对。

Requirements for a post-graduation work permit (PGWP)

For a post-graduation work permit, check school and program eligibility first, then the application window, language and field-of-study requirements.

5 figures and rules on this page, last checked 2026-10-17 · Written by our editorial team · Review in progress

Graduating from an eligible school does not mean every program automatically qualifies for a PGWP; time of enrolment, type of program, mode of study and public–private partnership arrangements each need checking.
Once the school releases final grades, the application window is 180 days, counted from when the school releases final grades
Source and check date

IRCC: official source

You have 180 days after your school issues your final marks to apply for a post-graduation work permit.

Effective: In force (page of 2026-09-28) · Checked: 2026-10-17 · ✅ primary source · Next review: 2027-04-15

. Whether you may keep working while waiting for graduation documents is governed by a separate set of status and authorization rules.
Language and field-of-study requirements already apply by program. Check the IRCC page directly before choosing a school; individual eligibility, submission and remedies should be assessed by a licensed immigration consultant or lawyer.

An eligible school still needs an eligible program

First confirm the school on the DLI list, then confirm whether the campus, the program and the time of enrolment allow a PGWP application. Transfers, distance learning and public–private arrangements may change the answer.
Keep the letter of acceptance, proof of enrolment, transcripts and completion letter. The date final grades are released starts the application window; do not count only from the graduation ceremony.

Language and field of study

The current language threshold for non-degree programs is CLB/NCLC 5, required in all four abilities

, and it is higher for university degree programs. Form of proof, validity of results and the special exemption: English or French test results are required and must be less than 2 years old on the day you apply; the only exemption is for graduates of PGWP-eligible flight schools

Status and work authorization before applying

The application window and lawful status are two separate matters. When the original study permit ends, whether you apply while it is valid, and when you must stop working under student conditions after graduating all have to be checked on your own timeline.
If documents are incomplete or your status has changed, do not infer from a general statement whether you can keep working. Keep the submission confirmation and school documents first, then have a licensed professional check.

Look first: Record the date the school officially released your final grades.

Common situations

The school released final grades before the graduation ceremony. The window runs from the release of grades, not from the ceremony.
You took a non-degree college program. Besides school and program eligibility, check the language threshold and the field-of-study list.
You plan to leave Canada after finishing your studies. First confirm your current status, documents for returning and work authorization; do not treat the PGWP application window as automatic permission to work.
