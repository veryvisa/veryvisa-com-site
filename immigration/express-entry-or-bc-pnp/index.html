快速通道看联邦邀请，省提名看本省需要；两条路径可以并行准备。

本页 24 个数字与规则，最近核对 2026-11-24 · 编辑部撰写 · 审定中

快速通道按综合排名与类别发出邀请；BC 省提名则先看省内项目条件、职业、雇主与工作经历。两者不是简单的快慢之分。
快速通道的分数与邀请人数会随轮次和类别变化，下方按 IRCC 公开记录完整列出；获得省提名后，仍要完成联邦永久居民审批。
选择路径前，先把语言、学历、职业、加拿大工作经历与雇主情况放在一起核对；个人资格与递交策略由持牌移民顾问或律师评估。

两条路径分别看什么

快速通道是联邦候选池。符合所管理项目的基本条件后，候选人进入池中，再按综合排名或类别邀请轮次等待邀请。
BC 省提名是省级路径。是否有合适类别，要看现行项目、职业、雇主资格、工作地点与申请人的经历；省里发出提名，不等于联邦审批已经完成。

先核对可改变的条件

语言成绩、学历认证与工作经历会影响联邦排名；合格雇主、职位与在省内工作的连续性，则常是省提名判断的重点。
同一家庭可以同时准备联邦候选档案与省提名材料，但两边的信息、职业代码与工作经历说明必须彼此一致。

怎样读下方记录

抽签记录用于理解近期邀请节奏，不是下一轮分数的预测。先看轮次类别，再看最低分与邀请人数，不能把不同类别混在一起比较。
BC 省提名的现行类别与邀请记录尚未纳入本页表格，申请前应直接核对省府页面。

Express Entry and BC provincial nomination: who each one suits

Express Entry turns on federal invitations, provincial nomination on the province's needs; the two routes can be prepared side by side.

24 figures and rules on this page, last checked 2026-11-24 · Written by our editorial team · Review in progress

Express Entry issues invitations by overall ranking and by category; BC provincial nomination looks first at provincial program conditions, occupation, employer and work experience. It is not simply a question of which is faster.
Express Entry scores and numbers of invitations vary by round and category; the full list below follows IRCC's public records. After a provincial nomination, federal permanent residence processing still has to be completed.
Before choosing a route, look at language, education, occupation, Canadian work experience and employer together. Personal eligibility and submission strategy should be assessed by a licensed immigration consultant or lawyer.

What each route looks at

Express Entry is the federal pool. After meeting the basic conditions of one of the programs it manages, a candidate enters the pool and waits for a general or category-based round of invitations.
BC provincial nomination is a provincial route. Whether a suitable category exists depends on the current program, the occupation, the employer's eligibility, the place of work and the applicant's experience. A nomination from the province does not mean federal approval is complete.

Check first what you can still change

Language results, credential assessment and work experience affect the federal ranking; a qualifying employer, the position and continuous work in the province are often what provincial nomination turns on.
A family can prepare a federal profile and provincial nomination documents at the same time, but the information, occupation codes and descriptions of experience on both sides must agree.

How to read the records below

Draw records help in understanding the recent rhythm of invitations; they do not predict the next score. Look at the round category first, then the lowest score and number of invitations, and do not compare different categories as if they were one.
BC's current nominee categories and invitation records are not yet included in this table; check the provincial pages directly before applying.

Knowledge cards

Knowledge cards

Two routes: federal and provincial nomination

Look at eligibility, invitation and approval separately

  1. Basic eligibility for the federal program
  2. The Express Entry pool
  3. Provincial nominee category and employer
  4. Federal permanent residence processing

Look first: Put language, education, occupation and work experience on one sheet first.

Easy to miss: Entering the pool is not the same as being invited, and being nominated by a province is not the same as becoming a permanent resident.

Next step: Check the federal ranking and the provincial categories separately under current conditions.

Takeaway: The two routes can run side by side, but what you declare in each must agree.

Knowledge cards

How to read draw records

Sort by category first, then look at scores and numbers

  1. Round category
  2. Date of invitations
  3. Lowest ranking score
  4. Invitations issued

Look first: Check whether it was a general round, a category-based round or a provincial nominee round.

Easy to miss: Lowest scores from different categories cannot simply be ranked against each other.

Next step: Compare your own profile with recent rounds of the same kind.

Takeaway: Past records show the rhythm; they do not predict the next round.

Common situations

You already have a qualifying employer and a steady job in BC, but your federal ranking is not high for now. Check provincial categories and the federal pool at the same time instead of waiting on one route.
Your language results are strong and your occupation falls within a federal invitation category, but you have no employer in the province. Check Express Entry program eligibility and category-based rounds first.
The job titles look alike, but the actual duties span different occupation codes. Settle the code by main duties first, then judge both routes, so that documents do not contradict each other.
