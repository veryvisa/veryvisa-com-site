怎样数天数

  1. 确定审查日，从这一天往前推出窗口的起点。
  2. 列出窗口期内每一次出境与入境的日期。
  3. 离开与回来的当天都算在加拿大；把其余在境外的天数从窗口总天数中扣除。
  4. 陪同加拿大公民配偶、同居伴侣或父母在境外的日子，以及全职受雇于加拿大企业、联邦公共行政部门或省级公共服务机构并被派驻境外的情形，按 IRCC 的条件单独列出并准备证明。

从审查日往前推，逐次扣除境外日

常见情形

成为永久居民后，有较长时间留在国内。还没满窗口年限的，看剩余时间里能否补足天数；已满的，从审查日往前数。
随加拿大公民配偶在国内生活。在境外共同居住的天数可能计入，要准备婚姻或同居关系与共同居住的证明，按 IRCC 的条件核对。
卡在国内时到期。身份不因卡到期而丧失；搭乘航班回加拿大，要先在境外申请旅行证件，申请时会审查居住义务。

How to count the days

  1. Fix the examination date and count back from it to the start of the window.
  2. List the date of every departure and entry within the window.
  3. The days you leave and return both count as days in Canada; subtract the other days abroad from the total days in the window.
  4. List separately, and prepare proof under IRCC's conditions for, days abroad accompanying a Canadian citizen spouse, common-law partner or parent, and full-time employment by a Canadian business, the federal public administration or a provincial public service that posted you abroad.

An expired card and your status

An expired card does not end permanent resident status. If you are outside Canada with an expired card, you need a permanent resident travel document (PRTD) to return by commercial carrier, and the residency obligation is examined when you apply.

Common situations

You spent a long time in China after becoming a permanent resident. If the length of the window has not yet passed, see whether the remaining time is enough to make up the days; if it has, count back from the examination date.
You live in China with your Canadian citizen spouse. Days living together abroad may count; prepare proof of the marriage or common-law relationship and of living together, and check it against IRCC's conditions.
The card expires while you are in China. Status is not lost because the card expired; to fly back to Canada you first apply for a travel document abroad, and the residency obligation is examined when you apply.
