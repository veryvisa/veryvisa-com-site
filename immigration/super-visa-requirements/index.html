Knowledge cards

Knowledge cards

Four groups of super visa documents

Relationship, income, insurance and entry

  1. Proof of relationship and invitation letter
  2. Family size and proof of income
  3. Medical insurance and proof of payment
  4. Passport, medical exam and visit plans

Look first: Draw the family relationships among the inviter, the co-signer and the visitor.

Easy to miss: An insurance quote cannot replace a valid policy and proof of payment.

Next step: Check the IRCC checklist in force on the day you apply.

Takeaway: All four groups have to hold at once.

Knowledge cards

The order for checking income

Family size first, then the year and the income that counts

  1. Determine family size
  2. Find the current threshold
  3. Choose the qualifying year
  4. Add the co-signer's income
  5. Check the conditions on the applicant's income

Look first: When family size changes, the minimum necessary income changes with it.

Easy to miss: The applicant's income cannot simply be added without checking the conditions.

Next step: Mark on each income document whose it is and for which year.

Takeaway: Sort income proof by person and year first.

Common situations

A couple invites one side's parents. Put both spouses' income documents and the family size on one sheet first, then check the co-signing conditions.
The parents have retirement income of their own. It cannot simply all be added to the inviter's side; first check the percentage the inviter has reached and the proof of the applicant's income.
The plan is to pay for insurance in instalments. Before submitting, confirm that the first instalment or deposit has been paid, and keep the valid policy and proof of payment for inspection on entry.
