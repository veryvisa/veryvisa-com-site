收入怎么报，费用怎么扣

副业的收入和费用按年汇总，在报税时附上那张表。能扣的是为赚取这笔收入而花的合理费用，例如材料、平台手续费、广告、专用设备的折旧；家用与私人开支不能扣。在家办公、自用车兼作生意用的，按使用比例分摊。
收入是不是生意收入，看是否持续、是否以营利为目的。偶尔卖掉自己的旧物通常不算；持续进货转卖、长期接单就要按生意报。收据与流水至少要能说明每一笔费用和生意有关。

什么时候必须注册 GST/HST

常见情形

上班族晚上接设计单，一年收入不高。按副业报收入与费用，工资里已交过退休金供款的，副业只补上限内的差额；销售远在门槛以下，不必注册销售税。
网店生意做大，某个季度一下子卖得很多。单季超过门槛的，超过那笔起就要收税，要尽快注册；不要等到年底再算。
刚开始做咨询，客户多是公司。可以考虑自愿注册：公司客户能抵扣您收的税，您也能抵回自己付过的税。

How income is reported and expenses deducted

Add up side income and expenses for the year and attach that form to your return. You can deduct reasonable expenses incurred to earn the income, such as materials, platform fees, advertising and depreciation on special equipment; household and personal spending cannot be deducted. For working at home or a personal car also used for business, split by use.
Whether income is business income depends on whether it is continuing and aims at profit. Selling your own used belongings now and then usually does not count; regularly buying goods to resell, or taking orders over a long time, is reported as a business. Receipts and bank records should at least show that each expense relates to the business.

Common situations

An employee takes design jobs in the evening, with modest income for the year. Report income and expenses as a side business; if CPP was already paid through wages, the side business only makes up the difference to the maximum. Sales are far below the threshold, so sales tax registration is not required.
An online shop grows, and one quarter's sales jump. Once a single quarter exceeds the threshold, tax is charged from the sale that went over and you should register quickly; do not wait until year-end.
You have just started consulting, mostly for companies. Consider registering voluntarily: company clients can claim back the tax you charge, and you can recover the tax you paid.
