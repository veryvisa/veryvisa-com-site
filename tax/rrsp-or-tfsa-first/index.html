收入在个人基本免税额以下、本来就不用交税时，RRSP 的扣除几乎没有作用，这时 TFSA 往往更合适，RRSP 的额度可以留到收入较高的年份再用。

退税的去处

RRSP 带来的退税如果直接花掉，比较就失去了意义。把退税再存进 TFSA，两种做法的结果才能放在一起看。

也要看福利

RRSP 的扣除会降低家庭净收入，按净收入计算的牛奶金可能因此增加；TFSA 的取款不算收入，退休后领 GIS 时不会因此减少，RRSP 或 RRIF 的提款则会计入收入。这两种效果都要放进同一笔账里。

If income is below the basic personal amount and no tax is due anyway, an RRSP deduction does almost nothing; a TFSA is often more suitable, and RRSP room can be saved for higher-income years.

Where the refund goes

If the refund from an RRSP is simply spent, the comparison loses its meaning. Only when the refund goes into a TFSA can the two results be set side by side.

Look at benefits too

An RRSP deduction lowers family net income, so a Canada Child Benefit based on net income may rise; TFSA withdrawals are not income and do not reduce GIS in retirement, while RRSP or RRIF withdrawals do count. Both effects belong in the same sum.

Which to fund first: compare two tax rates

Your marginal rate now and your rate in retirement

  1. Higher now than in retirement: RRSP ahead
  2. Lower now than in retirement: TFSA ahead
  3. Close to equal: look at flexibility and benefits
  4. A fair comparison reinvests the tax refund

Look first: Estimate your yearly income sources in retirement: pensions, account withdrawals, rent.

Easy to miss: If income is too low to owe tax, a deduction is worth almost nothing.

Next step: When comparing, include the case where the refund goes into the TFSA.

Takeaway: The level of the two rates decides which is better.

Knowledge cards

TFSA room after a withdrawal

The amount withdrawn is added back the next year

  1. Withdraw this year
  2. Putting it back this year uses remaining room
  3. Room is restored at the start of next year
  4. Over-contributions are penalized by the month

Look first: Before putting money back in the same year, check your remaining room.

Easy to miss: Moving between banks by withdrawing and redepositing also uses room.

Next step: Go by the room shown in your CRA account.

Takeaway: A TFSA is flexible, but room comes back by the year.

Common situations

It is your first year after landing, with little income. With no Canadian earned income last year, no new RRSP room has been created yet; TFSA room starts to build from the year you are both a tax resident and an adult.
One spouse earns more than the other. The higher earner can look into a spousal RRSP, which places later withdrawals in the lower earner's name; but if the contributor paid into a spousal RRSP in the year of withdrawal or the two calendar years before, the attribution rule counts the withdrawal as the contributor's income: if the contributing spouse paid into any spousal RRSP in the year of withdrawal or the two previous tax years, all or part of the withdrawal is included in the contributor's income
